Services

Garden work, priced before it starts.

Hedge trimming, tree works, maintenance and design, decorative gravel and fencing. Competitive prices across the North West.

01

Hedge trimming

Shaping, a seasonal cut, or a proper reduction in height and width. The aim is a clean boundary, not a hedge left bristling.

  • Privet, laurel, beech, leylandii and mixed boundary hedges
  • Sides, tops and a tidy line where the hedge meets the path
  • Clippings cleared rather than left on the lawn

If a hedge is too tall for a straightforward visit, or it hangs over a road, we say so before quoting.

Quote for hedge trimming
02

Tree works

Pruning, crown tidy, lifting low branches, taking out small unwanted growth, and clearing storm debris.

  • A look at access before a price is agreed
  • Smaller garden trees and overhanging growth
  • An honest stop if the tree is protected or needs a tree surgeon

We do not guess at a large or dangerous tree. That kind of job is pointed out on the visit.

Quote for tree work
03

Garden maintenance

Grass, borders, weeding, leaves and seasonal cut-backs. One visit, or a regular round that neighbours can rely on.

  • Lawns and edges
  • Beds, weeds and plants that have got ahead of themselves
  • A repeat visit if the garden needs a steady hand
Quote for maintenance
04

Garden design

Practical layouts for real North West gardens: beds, a place to sit, a path that makes sense, and planting that can live with the weather.

  • A simple plan you can follow, or that we can build
  • Ideas that suit the space you already have
  • Design on its own, or design that leads into the work
Quote for design
05

Decorative gravel

Gravel gardens, paths and tired surfaces brought back. Edging and a membrane are talked through so the gravel stays where it was put.

  • New gravel areas and refreshed paths
  • Edging so stones do not walk into the lawn
  • A finish that suits the rest of the garden
Quote for gravel
06

Fencing

Panel replacement, repairs, new runs and gates, where access allows. Lines and levels are checked before the quote, not after the panels are up.

  • Broken panels and leaning posts
  • New runs along a boundary
  • Gates where the opening is straightforward

Questions

Before you book

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in the North West and visit gardens across Greater Manchester, Lancashire, Merseyside and Cheshire, including Manchester, Liverpool, Chester, Preston, Warrington and nearby towns.

How do your quotes work?

Tell us about the job by phone, WhatsApp or the quote form. We confirm a clear price before work starts, so the cost is not a surprise afterwards.

What garden work do you do?

Hedge trimming, tree work, general garden maintenance and design, decorative gravel and fencing. If a job needs a specialist, we will say so on the visit.

Can you come back on a regular basis?

Yes. Garden maintenance can be a one-off tidy or a repeat visit. Tell us how often the garden needs a hand when you enquire.

How do I contact you?

Call or WhatsApp 07348 426 709, or send the quote form. We use those details only to reply to your enquiry.
