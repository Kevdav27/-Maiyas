About

A local service, easy to reach.

Maiya's Gardening Renovation looks after North West gardens for people who want the work done carefully, priced clearly, and simple to book.

What we stand by

Careful work, a clear price.

The work is hedge trimming, tree work, general garden maintenance and design, decorative gravel and fencing. Prices are competitive, and the quote is agreed before the tools come out.

We are based in the North West. Visits cover Greater Manchester, Lancashire, Merseyside, Cheshire and the towns around them.

You can call or WhatsApp 07348 426 709. A person answers the enquiry, not a call centre.

How we work

Straight about the job

Look first

Access, height and what is growing all change the price. We would rather see the garden than guess from a photo alone.

Price first

You get a clear figure before work starts, so a competitive quote stays the price you agreed.

Say if it is not ours

Protected trees, dangerous timber, or a job that needs a specialist are flagged on the visit rather than forced through.
