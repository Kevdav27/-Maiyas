Privacy

How your details are used

Maiya's Gardening Renovation, North West England. Phone 07348 426 709. This notice was written for this website on 29 September 2026.

Who we are

Maiya's Gardening Renovation is a local garden service. For privacy questions, call or WhatsApp 07348 426 709. Add a postal address to this page when you publish one.

What you can send us

If you use the contact or quote form, you may give your name, phone number, email address, postcode and a description of the garden. If you call or WhatsApp, we receive whatever you choose to say.

What this website does not do

The forms do not send those details to a server we control, and this site does not run advertising or analytics cookies. After the checks on the page, your browser opens WhatsApp with a draft. You send that message yourself. WhatsApp is provided by Meta, under its own terms.

The page may keep a timestamp in your browser's session storage so a double-click does not open several drafts. That timestamp is not your message, and it is not sent to us.

Why we use the details

Only to reply to your enquiry or quote, arrange a visit, and do the work you agree. We do not sell the details.

How long they stay

A WhatsApp chat stays until you or we delete it. If you would like an enquiry deleted, contact us on the number above and say so.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you think your details have been misused.
